## Setup notes

**Timing:** Email 1 sends immediately on signup. Emails 2-5 send at 10:00 AM in the subscriber's timezone (or Pacific if unknown) on days 3, 6, 10, and 14.

**Segmentation (future):** Once you have enough subscribers, segment by source:
- `wellness-assessment` leads get an extra email about their specific pain categories
- `employer-roi-calculator` leads get employer-specific follow-ups (they're likely HR)
- `401k-calculator` / `debt-payoff` leads get tool-specific deep dives

**Unsubscribe:** Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe. Required by law, and also just decent.

**Reply-to:** Set to tony@12thandgood.com so replies actually reach you. Real replies from real leads are worth more than open rates.

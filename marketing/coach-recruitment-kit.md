## The one-sentence pitch

**"A fee-only marketplace that sends you warm, ready-to-work clients who share your background — and never asks you to sell a product to anyone."**

Everything below is that sentence, expanded for different audiences.

---

## 1. Why a great coach actually says yes

Most recruiting pitches talk about the platform. Coaches don't care about the platform — they care about their practice, their income, and their conscience. Lead with those three.

**You keep your independence and your conscience.** No products, no commissions, no AUM quotas, no compliance pressure to steer a client toward something that pays you. You give the advice you'd give your own family. For a lot of good people who left big firms *because* of that pressure, this is the whole reason to join.

**We bring you clients who already want coaching.** Our Money Blueprint quiz and employer partnerships put pre-qualified people in front of you who have already raised their hand. You spend your time coaching, not marketing. That's the hardest, most expensive part of running a solo practice — and we carry it.

**You serve your own community.** Most of our clients are people of color building wealth without a family playbook. A client working with someone who has lived their reality changes what's possible in the room. If "be the advisor I wish my family had" means something to you, this is that.

**The admin is handled.** Scheduling, payments, video sessions, and an AI assistant that helps clients prep — all built in. You show up to the conversation; we run the plumbing.

**Being selective is the point.** We vet every coach and we turn people away. That's not a hoop — it's your credibility. "Vetted, fee-only, conflict-free" is a claim clients can trust *because* not everyone gets in.

---

## 2. Who we're looking for (the ideal coach)

Use this as your own filter, and paste a trimmed version into job posts.

- **Credentialed or credibly experienced.** CFP, AFC, CFEI, ChFC, an accounting/financial background, or a track record coaching real people through real money decisions. Letters help but lived competence counts.
- **Fee-only in spirit.** Believes advice shouldn't be attached to a product sale. If they currently earn on commissions/AUM, they need to be comfortable leaving that at the door here.
- **Shares the community they'd serve.** The differentiator is lived experience. We especially want Black coaches and coaches of color who can meet clients where they are.
- **Clean record.** No serious regulatory or disciplinary history. We check.
- **Coach, not lecturer.** Warm, plain-spoken, judgment-free. Someone who can sit with a client's real numbers and real shame and make the next step feel doable.

---

## 3. How we vet (so "vetted" means something)

Have this ready — good coaches will ask, and the rigor is a selling point, not a deterrent.

1. **Application + credentials check** — verify letters, licenses, and background.
2. **Regulatory/background screen** — confirm a clean disciplinary and compliance record.
3. **Values & fit conversation** — a real interview about how they coach and why the conflict-free model matters to them.
4. **Sample session** — watch them actually coach (a mock or recorded session). This is where great coaches separate themselves.
5. **Onboarding** — profile, rates, availability, and a short norms/quality walkthrough before they go live.

## 5. Objections you'll hear (and honest answers)

**"Do I have to give up my own practice?"**
No. Most coaches keep their practice and take 12th & Good clients alongside it. We're a client channel, not an employer.

**"How do clients actually find me?"**
Two ways: people take our Money Blueprint quiz and get matched to coaches who fit them, and employers bring their teams onto the platform. You're not cold-marketing — clients arrive already wanting help.

**"How and when do I get paid?"**
You set your rates. Clients pay up front through the platform; you're paid out [PAYOUT TIMING]. Our fee is [PLATFORM FEE MODEL]. (Lock these before sending — see below.)

**"Is this only for Black coaches and Black clients?"**
Our focus is serving people underserved by traditional finance — that's disproportionately people of color, and lived-experience matching is core to what we do. It's not exclusionary; it's intentional about who we're built to serve well.

**"What does it cost me to join?"**
Nothing to apply or list. We only make money when you do — [PLATFORM FEE MODEL].

**"What's the catch on 'conflict-free'?"**
There isn't one — that *is* the product. There's nothing else for sale. The session is what the client pays for, and that's what makes the conversation different.

## 6. Numbers to lock before you send these

The templates are ready except for a few specifics only you can set. Nail these down first so you're never caught mid-pitch:

- **Platform fee / revenue split** — what percentage or flat fee does 12th & Good take per session?
- **Payout timing** — when does a coach get paid after a session (e.g., weekly, net-X, instant)?
- **Cost to join** — confirmed free to apply/list? Any subscription for coaches?
- **Launch roster target** — how many coaches for launch? (Keep it tight — a curated 8–15 beats a sparse 50.)
- **Rate guidance** — do coaches set rates freely, or within a suggested band?
